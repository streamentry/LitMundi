# Governance and continuity

## Principles and present stewardship

Textual integrity, public access, attribution, literary fidelity, editorial independence, lawful reuse and portability precede corpus growth. The GitHub repository owner, **streamentry**, is the sole known steward. No external institution, mirror custodian, successor or reviewer is represented as having accepted a role.

A future handoff should name responsibilities and obtain each person's consent in a reviewed record. More independent maintainers and custodians are desirable; they are not an institution already in place.

## Decisions and publication

Architectural decisions belong in [ADRs](docs/adr/README.md); literary choices, alternatives and disagreements belong with the edition. Published corrections use reviewable PRs and preserve stable identifiers. Source provenance and earlier assessments remain available rather than being silently overwritten.

Every translated publication requires **two independent qualified human reviewers**, each ≥9.0 overall, fidelity ≥9.0 and every other dimension ≥8.0, complete coverage and no unresolved major/critical error. Reviewers disclose qualifications, language competence and conflicts. The final source/target hashes must match their reviews. A separate maintainer attestation records verification of identities, independence, evidence, rights and affirmative contributor consent. CI checks the declarations and hashes; it cannot authenticate people or certify literary merit.

AI drafts and scores never supply human approval or license permission. The three current Vietnamese drafts have neither. See [Quality](docs/QUALITY.md) and [Editorial workflow](docs/EDITORIAL-WORKFLOW.md).

## Rights and disputes

The MIT software/documentation license does not license the literary corpus. Respect each edition's recorded rights and the moral rights applicable to its authors. Do not infer a blanket CC0 grant, relicense human contributions without permission or imply that possession of an editorial archive authorizes draft publication.

Report a textual or rights concern through the repository's issue channel; use [Security](SECURITY.md) for private security disclosures. For a credible rights dispute, withhold the disputed edition from public publication while the maintainer investigates. Preserve provenance, the reported concern and resolution history to the extent lawful. Do not treat an AI legal assessment as conclusive authority.

## If the original maintainer becomes unavailable

Another organization can continue the work without the original domain or GitHub account:

1. Obtain a lawful repository copy or editorial archive and independently retained digest. Follow the [offline restoration procedure](docs/PRESERVATION.md), documenting the result and provenance of the copy.
2. Inspect the per-edition rights manifest. Reuse software/docs under MIT and literary content only under its actual rights or license. Keep uncleared drafts clearly separated; obtain permission or make a new lawful translation before publishing them.
3. Preserve canonical work, edition and passage IDs, source citations, attribution, licensing and review history. A new translation or different source witness receives a new edition ID; do not reuse an old identity for a replacement.
4. Recheck publication evidence and applicable jurisdictional rights. A valid checksum does not make a false attestation true. Recruit accountable reviewers and document the successor organization's own maintainer decisions.
5. Publish on any suitable static host, state clearly that it is a successor or independent fork, and record custody and continuity. Access to the old account, domain or Git history is helpful but is not needed to read the exported corpus.

This is a lawful technical continuation path, not an automatic transfer of trademarks, domains, accounts or private rights. The current project has no signed succession agreement and no claimed external mirror. GitHub's 30-day CI artifact retention does not satisfy independent custody.

## Sustainable scope

Publish a valuable reviewed edition, obtain actual reader feedback and measure editorial effort before commissioning more work. Record real custodians and restoration dates once they exist. If funding is collected later, publish its conditions and basic accounts; no funding body or council is implied by this policy.
