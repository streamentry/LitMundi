# Governance and continuity
## Principles
Text integrity, public access, attribution, literary fidelity, editorial independence, legal clarity and portability precede growth or advertising revenue.

## Decision process
Maintainers record architectural and policy decisions in ADRs; translation decisions must retain evidence and review history. Two independent language-qualified reviewers should approve an edition before it is labeled expert-reviewed; conflicts of interest are disclosed. No single maintainer may silently revise published editions.

## Stewardship
The owner currently administers the repo. A sustainable project needs at least three independent maintainers, documented succession, mirror custodians, dispute resolution and periodic public financial reports if donations or ads are collected. This is a target, **not an existing institution**.

## Rights
No exclusive relicensing of community translations without author consent; archive export and forkability are protected. Maintain a publicly discoverable process for takedown/rights disputes, corrections, and reversions.
