# ADR-0004: Preservation and rights boundaries

Status: Accepted — 2026-10-31

## Decision
License code and docs under MIT, keep content rights independent and per-edition. Provide archive exports/checksums. Preserve attribution, review provenance and source rights status. Treat legal uncertainty as a blocker for publishing, not an optional disclaimer.

## Consequences
Cannot bulk-ingest copyrighted modern editions. More work per book; stronger trust and reproducibility.
