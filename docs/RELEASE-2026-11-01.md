## What readers can use

The existing Astro, GitHub Pages and canonical JSON architecture remains in place. The release provides English, Vietnamese, French and Spanish interfaces; language/edition selection with original-language fallback; author and work navigation; stable passage links and correction context; search over published text; optional browser-local saved reading position; and JSON, TXT, TSV and EPUB exports. Reading, language navigation and download links work without JavaScript.

The reader supports explicit many-to-many original/translation alignment. Because no translation has qualified for publication, that mode was verified with a clearly fictional fixture outside the canonical corpus. The deployed reading catalog contains the three originals below. Canonical URLs and existing passage identities remain preserved; legacy demonstrations are non-indexed.

## Quality and publication controls

Every v1 weight and threshold remains unchanged: **35/25/15/15/10**, two independent human scores of at least **9.0 each**, fidelity at least **9.0**, every other dimension at least **8.0**, full coverage and no unresolved major/critical finding.

The validator now uses full JSON Schema validation and checks alignment, stable identifiers, current source/target hashes, source evidence, rights, direct review evidence, reviewer qualifications/conflicts and a maintainer's exact-hash attestation. Honest negative and superseded reviews remain inspectable without becoming approvals. CI checks declarations and consistency; a real maintainer must authenticate people, permissions and literary judgments. Blank review templates deliberately contain no asserted coverage, scores or approval.

The [editorial workflow](EDITORIAL-WORKFLOW.md) connects discovery, source rights, ingestion, drafting, AI critique, independent review, revision, publication, corrections and preservation with explicit entry/exit conditions.

## Preservation and remaining human work

The deterministic editorial archive carries canonical UTF-8 JSON, schemas, provenance, edition-specific rights, review history, labelled drafts, published portable formats, SHA-256/byte manifests and a standalone Python restorer. It reconstructs readable HTML/TXT without Astro, GitHub, a network connection or the original domain. Draft possession grants no publication license. [Preservation instructions](PRESERVATION.md), [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md) and ADRs [0007](adr/0007-review-history-and-technical-source-checks.md)/[0008](adr/0008-portable-publications-and-offline-restoration.md) document the boundaries and succession procedure.

GitHub Actions retains the archive for **30 days**. No independent custodian, permanent mirror or institutional restoration result has been obtained. Those acceptance criteria remain open in [#4](https://github.com/streamentry/LitMundi/issues/4).

[Reviewer recruitment #13](https://github.com/streamentry/LitMundi/issues/13) requests genuine bilingual reviewers and human editorial/license contributions. The first reviewed translation milestone [#2](https://github.com/streamentry/LitMundi/issues/2) remains open. The deployed reader feedback form asks for actual reading completion, voluntary return, useful corrections and recommendation intent; there are no measured reader outcomes yet, so demand validation remains open in [#3](https://github.com/streamentry/LitMundi/issues/3).

**Single highest-value next action:** secure two qualified independent English–Vietnamese literary reviewers for the complete five-passage Wilde draft, together with a responsible human editor's affirmative open-license permission. Their independent, passage-grounded feedback should drive the next revision and publication decision before any larger translation pipeline is built.
