# Pilot 003 — Emilia Pardo Bazán, “El encaje roto”

Status, 2026-11-01: **complete historical Spanish source edition available; complete Vietnamese draft withheld from public reading and downloads**. Human literary review, contributor attribution and open-license permission have not been obtained.

## Why this work and what is not established

This small pilot adds direct Spanish-to-Vietnamese work and a woman author's account of a bride's refusal. Its central demands are concrete: distinguish imagined spectacle from eyewitness testimony, preserve social irony, keep the narrator's long accumulating sentences, and avoid softening or diagnosing the fiancé's displayed hostility.

The [Translation Gap Atlas](../research/translation-gap-atlas.json) records the selection evidence. Exact Vietnamese and Indonesian editions were not located in its bounded searches; neither the absence of translation nor reader demand has been verified. Selection is for a limited editorial feasibility test with an accessible historical witness. It is not permission to scale a translation pipeline.

Every page was visually compared with the new transcription by AI-assisted agents. The canonical JSON has **15 printed paragraphs**, including the long ceremony descriptions; all are translated and explicitly aligned.

The approximate date in brackets is a catalog judgment, not a claimed year printed in the volume. The story's 19 September 1897 appearance in *El Liberal* is recorded in the [Cervantes transcription](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra-visor/cuentos-de-amor--0/html/fee33ed8-82b1-11df-acc7-002185ce6064_3.html). ## Rights assessment

The author died in 1921, as the named catalog record confirms. Spain's older rule matters: [BNE's explanation](https://consultas.bne.es/faq/267300) and [TRLPI transitional provision 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930) preserve the earlier life-plus-80 term for authors who died before 7 December 1987. That term has expired for Pardo Bazán, including under the later end-of-calendar-year calculation. The assessment does not assume life plus 70 is sufficient for every Spanish author.

Vietnam's [ordinary literary life-plus-50 term](https://cov.gov.vn/quyen-tac-gia-quyen-lien-quan/thoi-han-bao-ho-quyen-tac-gia-la-bao-nhieu-nam-168808.html) has also expired. In the US, even a restored foreign copyright would receive only the [remainder of the ordinary pre-1978 term](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ38b.pdf); these nineteenth-century publication dates exceed that term. Sources and jurisdictions are recorded per edition.

Only the historical literary text is reproduced. The source's public-domain status does not grant rights in a modern translation, an editor's new material, a library's page design or scan images. Those materials are not redistributed. Moral rights, attribution and textual integrity remain relevant. The Vietnamese draft has **no cleared publication license** and no named human editorial contributor; those cannot be supplied by a source-term calculation.

## Literary revision and remaining questions

The [editorial dossier](../corpus/editorial/pardo-bazan-el-encaje-roto/pardo-encaje-vi-ai-draft-2026.json) preserves a ten-entry glossary, alternatives, rejected proposals and source/target hashes. It records corrections from separate AI-assisted readings:

- The unmarked phrase *tiếng không rõ ràng* could invert the meaning by reading as “an unclear sound.” Spoken **“không”** and **“có”** are now quoted, reflecting the source's typographic emphasis.
- *Violento* was initially softened to *nóng nảy*. It is now *tính khí hung bạo*, without inventing a witnessed physical assault or using a clinical label.
- *No llegó á tanto* now says that he did not utter those words. The earlier *chưa* falsely suggested “not yet.”
- *Dureza* is now *khắc nghiệt*, not the potentially admirable firmness of *cứng rắn*.
- *Medio ambiente* now retains the wider social setting rather than narrowing it to the audience alone.

The narrator did not attend the wedding; the ceremony remains her imagined scene, and Micaelita's explanation arrives three years later. This distinction must survive every future edit. Her right to refuse is not turned into a fabricated diagnosis or an omniscient report of Bernardo's future conduct.

Qualified Spanish–Vietnamese reviewers still need to resolve these choices:

1. **Jewelry.** *Roca antigua* is provisionally rendered as antique precious stones. Its exact historical material/cut and singular or collective force need a better historical witness; the [modern RAE entry](https://dle.rae.es/roca) does not establish a specific jewelry sense. Do not confidently relabel it as diamonds or rock crystal without evidence.
2. **Historical measure.** The draft retains *tercia*. [RAE defines it as one third of a vara](https://dle.rae.es/tercio); a precise centimeter conversion requires a specific regional standard. Decide whether to add a reader note, rather than silently insert a modern measurement.
3. **Idiom and cadence.** *Un decir Jesús* currently retains both the momentary sense and the religious utterance. A human editor should judge whether this explanatory doubling harms Micaelita's conversational voice. The long social tableau needs a Vietnamese read-aloud edit without erasing its deliberately accumulating structure.
